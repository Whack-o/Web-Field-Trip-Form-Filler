How to Use the Field Trip Form Filler

  1. Upload a spreadsheet with cadet information. Required columns: Student ID, First Name, Last Name, Grade, Phone Number, Address. Blank cells are fine.
  2. Enter a name for the output PDF. When you generate, the file downloads to your device.
  3. Search for cadets using any identifying information. Click a result to add them to your list.
  4. Click Generate Form to create your field trip packet with filled participant information.

This app only fills participant fields (same PDF method as the desktop app). Review the PDF for missing spreadsheet values or typos. Your spreadsheet and selected list are saved locally in this browser. The first Generate may take longer while the PDF engine loads.
